## Kept from ChatGPT
- Follow ONE business (סטודיו שיער דנה) from a pin on the map to a sent quote, instead of six separate job nodes.
  Mirrors the landing walkthrough "מנקודה על המפה ללקוח משלם".
- Payoff headline "מהמפה להצעת מחיר, בלוח אחד."
- Lines: "פותחים את הכרטיס של דנה. ליד המספר כבר כתוב מה להגיד." and the Telegram line.

## Changed (Claude's review)
1. Hook opens on the RESULT, then rewinds (the owner's rule after the LifeMaxer reel and the process film): frame 1
   is the sent quote card; ChatGPT opened on the question only, with "מצאתם עסק" contradicting "the system finds it".
2. Cut the website-filter beat ("רק עסקים בלי אתר"): it makes LeadFlow look like a tool for web designers, and the
   owner said "its a crm for anything" (ChatGPT flagged this risk itself).
3. Close on the benefit (landing: "לא לאבד אף אחת, ולחזור בזמן שאמרת"), not on "you send the quote by email".
4. "בשלישי בעשר" kept as the sample time; "כל יום" for the scan (no claim about mornings).
