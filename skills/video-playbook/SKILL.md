# Video playbook

10 lessons from 5 sources, last changed 2026-11-19. This is a snapshot, taken on 2026-11-25, of the playbook
the two reels in this repo followed.
The owner sends marketing videos and posts to their CRM, the video PC studies each one, and the owner keeps the
lessons worth keeping. Many examples are from the owner's LifeMaxer app account: take the technique, then add your
own lessons as your videos teach you.

## How to use it
- Read all of it before you plan a video, and apply every lesson that fits this video and its format.
- The owner's rules win over this playbook: the project's rules, the brand's voice and off-limits list, and
  whatever the owner said about this video. Never invent a fact, number or result to follow a lesson.
- "N sources agree" means several marketers said it. When two lessons pull different ways, prefer the one
  with more sources, or the one the owner's own results back.
- Take the technique, not the source's words, music or footage.
- When you report on a video, name the lesson numbers you applied ("Playbook #3, #7").

## Hook (the first 3 seconds)

- **#1** Open with a fixed two-line meme setup, 'Them: "<promise>" / Them 6 months later:', and cut to the app as the punchline by 1.1 seconds.
  - Why: The caption sets up the joke and the app finishes it, so the product feels like the payoff rather than an ad. With 23.3K likes, the format works with no voice at all.
  - Looks like: Frame 1 shows 'Him: "I'll start Monday" / Him 6 months later:' in big words, then at 1 s it cuts to a LifeMaxer screen labelled 'Example' with a 180-day streak.
  - From ["I'm going to marry her" / 6 months later: habit tracker meme reel](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DU_BMdDk3Ur/)
- **#2** Open on big text or the real app on frame 1, never on AI-generated people; bring a person in only after the hook has landed.
  - Why: On @lifemaxer_app the two reels that opened on AI people were skipped 78% (twins) and 88% (UGC ad); the two text-led openings 46% (21-days) and 64% (Wrapped).
  - Looks like: Twins reel: frame 1 is a big "SAME GOALS. 6 MONTHS LATER." card over two Life Scores, and the twins appear at 0:01.
  - From Instagram insights 18 Nov: twins and Wrapped reels · used in 5 videos
- **#10** Open on one uncut shot of something visibly about to happen, with no text for the first 2 to 3 seconds, and land the impact around 3 seconds.
  - Why: The creator says showing what's about to happen and making viewers wait means their brain needs the ending, so they keep watching.
  - Looks like: Frame 1 shows the real app with a 99-day streak counter as the mascot's finger slowly reaches for the 'reset' button, and at 3 s it hits 100 instead.
  - From [3 anticipation hooks that keep people watching (and how to use them)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfkdfanO2S7/)

## Story

- **#3** Say or show the brand name only in the last few seconds; until then show the app inside the story without naming it.
  - Why: Wrapped named LifeMaxer at 0:04 and fell from about 37% to 30% watching; the 21-days reel dropped from 20% to 8% the second its app pitch started at 0:36.
  - Looks like: Wrapped: "Your month, wrapped" over the app's screens, and "LifeMaxer" only on the end card.
  - From Instagram insights 18 Nov: twins and Wrapped reels, [MyHours: daily routine planner, "POV: You turn your life into a game"](https://www.tiktok.com/@bursinru/video/7582335328350965013) · 2 sources agree · used in 5 videos
- **#4** Open with a question the viewer wants answered, raise it again in the middle beats, and answer it fully only in the final 3 to 5 seconds.
  - Why: The creator says a hook that asks a question, a buildup that strengthens it and a payoff that answers it is what makes people watch to the end.
  - Looks like: A LifeMaxer reel opens on 'Why do twins end up 6 months apart?', the middle shows the gap growing, and the app screen that explains it lands in the last 4 seconds.
  - From [Listen to your retention graph: hook, buildup or payoff?](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfrEJGFTZ7J/) · used in 4 videos

## Pacing

- **#5** In the buildup, add a new reason to keep watching every few seconds (a new fact, stake or visual) and cut any beat that only repeats.
  - Why: His on-screen fix for a slowly sliding graph is 'strengthen the question, don't drag it out, re-hook every few seconds'.
  - Looks like: In a 30 s reel, each kinetic line from 4 s to 25 s adds a new number or twist, such as 'Day 30: Jake skipped 9 workouts', instead of restating the problem.
  - From [Listen to your retention graph: hook, buildup or payoff?](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfrEJGFTZ7J/) · used in 5 videos

## Visuals

- **#6** When showing a screen or chart, pin it in the top third and draw a red arrow onto the exact spot the narrator names, at the moment the word is spoken.
  - Why: The creator points at the exact place on the graph with a hand-drawn arrow and a big label, so viewers instantly see what he means.
  - Looks like: An app screen labelled 'Example' sits at the top, and a red arrow sweeps onto the streak counter as the narrator says 'streak', with a bold 'STREAK' label underneath.
  - From [Listen to your retention graph: hook, buildup or payoff?](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfrEJGFTZ7J/) · used in 4 videos

## On-screen text

- **#9** Use one 'POV: you <become someone better>' line of about 7 words in the top fifth, unchanged from frame 1 to the end.
  - Why: The single fixed line 'POV: You turn your life into a game' frames a plain app demo as a wish the viewer has, and nothing else on screen needs to change.
  - Looks like: 'POV: you finally stick to your habits' stays fixed above a LifeMaxer screen recording while the checkmarks fill in below it.
  - From [MyHours: daily routine planner, "POV: You turn your life into a game"](https://www.tiktok.com/@bursinru/video/7582335328350965013)
- **#11** Right after the hook's payoff, freeze or slow the impact frame, darken it about 40%, and put the narrator's first line over it in big kinetic words.
  - Why: The payoff image stays on screen as a backdrop while the explanation starts, so the step from hook to topic never shows an empty or new frame.
  - Looks like: The streak counter freezes on '100', dims, and 'THIS is what 100 days looks like' builds word by word in the centre.
  - From [3 anticipation hooks that keep people watching (and how to use them)](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfkdfanO2S7/) · used in 1 videos

## Other

- **#7** Read each video's retention graph: a sharp drop in the first 3 seconds means fix the hook, a steady slide means the middle, and a late drop means the payoff.
  - Why: The creator says the shape of the watch-time curve shows which of the three parts failed and what to fix next.
  - Looks like: If a LifeMaxer reel loses half its viewers by 2 s, the next version only changes the frame-1 line and the narrator's first words, and keeps the rest.
  - From [Listen to your retention graph: hook, buildup or payoff?](https://www.instagram.com/reel/DfrEJGFTZ7J/) · used in 1 videos
