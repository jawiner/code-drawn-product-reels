# Brief: voice script for a 45-second Hebrew reel about LeadFlow

You are a senior short-form video scriptwriter. Write the voice-over script (in Hebrew) and on-screen text for a
vertical Instagram/TikTok reel about LeadFlow, a CRM for small Israeli sellers. Then critique your own draft.

## The product (facts you may use; do not invent anything else)
LeadFlow: "ה־CRM שמוצא לך לקוחות חדשים". "לכל מי שמוכר, לא משנה מה" (websites, insurance, equipment, a monthly service).
- One board for leads, calls, follow-up and quotes ("פניות, שיחות, מעקב והצעות מחיר בלוח אחד").
- Daily scan on Google Maps: you choose business types and a city, minimum rating and number of reviews, and whom to
  keep: everyone, only businesses with no website, or only those whose website looks outdated (with the reasons).
- The scan checks results against your leads: businesses you already have are skipped; new ones land in
  "ליצירת קשר" with phone number, address and rating. Landing-page example: "מספרות · חיפה · 4.5★ ומעלה" →
  "נמצאו 9 · נוספו 4" (3 rated lower and 2 already in your leads were skipped).
- A call script appears next to the phone number. After the call you mark the result ("ענה") and set a reminder
  on the same screen.
- Telegram reminder (and a computer alert) when it's time to call back, with the latest notes, the best way to
  reach the person and an opening line, e.g. title "הגיע הזמן להתקשר לסטודיו שיער דנה", opening line
  "שלום דנה, רציתי לחזור אלייך בקשר לסטודיו שיער דנה. זה זמן טוב לדבר?". Done / snooze buttons in the message.
- Quote calculator: tick items from your own products and prices; the quote adds itself up (one-time + monthly),
  goes out by email, and the lead moves to "הצעה נשלחה".
- Won deal: the lead becomes a customer and the project opens.
- "היום" page: every morning one list of whom to call, overdue call-backs first, a counter of today's calls vs. your goal.
- Also (minor): Sales Lab (practice the call with an AI playing the business owner, then a score), a work report on
  Telegram (calls, who answered, deals, money in), an AI-written first email per lead.
- Works on the phone; Hebrew and English.
- Status: NOT open yet. "החשבונות נפתחים בקרוב", early access ("לבקש גישה מוקדמת"), sign-up by WhatsApp message.
- The pain it replaces (from the landing page): today it's scattered between Google Maps tabs, an Excel sheet,
  WhatsApp and notes on the phone. "כל פנייה היא מכירה שמחכה לך... העבודה היא לא לאבד אף אחת, ולחזור בזמן שאמרת."

All names and numbers in the film are SAMPLES and are labelled "לדוגמה" on screen (Dana Hair Studio / סטודיו שיער
דנה, Haifa hair salons, 4.6★ from 87 reviews, Tuesday 10:00, ₪3,300 + ₪450 a month). Never claim results, customer
counts, revenue gains or testimonials.

## The format
- Drawn in code (no people, no stock footage): a dark sci-fi HUD world with a moving 3D camera, like a sister film
  we already made for another product (an AI phone receptionist). One continuous space; each "job" of the product
  lights up as a node with a short title (2-3 words) and a sub-line, and the camera moves from job to job.
- A small tag "נפתח בקרוב · הרשמה מוקדמת" at the top from 4 s to the end.
- Hebrew AI voice (female, warm, plain everyday Hebrew, not salesy), talking almost all the time. Captions of the
  voice are burned in. Music bed underneath.
- 40-48 seconds, 9:16. About 10-11 short lines of voice, each 2.5-4.5 s.
- End card: "LeadFlow" + "נפתח בקרוב" + button "לבקש גישה מוקדמת בוואטסאפ".

## The owner's rules for videos (apply all)
1. Open on big text or the real product UI on frame 1; never on people.
2. Say or show the brand name (LeadFlow) ONLY on the end card / last seconds. Before that, show the product without naming it.
3. A question hook works: open with a question the viewer wants answered, raise it again in the middle, answer it fully
   only in the last 3-5 seconds.
4. In the middle, every few seconds add a NEW reason to keep watching (a new fact, stake or visual). Cut any line
   that only repeats.
5. An anticipation hook also works: one uncut shot of something visibly about to happen, no text for the first
   2-3 seconds, the impact around 3 s.
6. Plain words a non-technical shop owner understands. No jargon, no hype words, no "revolutionary", no "AI-powered".
7. Address the viewer in plural "אתם" (the sister film and the agency site do).

## Our draft beats for LeadFlow (improve them freely)
hook → 01 "מוצאת לקוחות בגוגל מפות" (search, pins light up) → 02 "רק מי שחדש" (already-yours fade, 4 new land on the
board) → 03 "לוח אחד" (leads, calls, follow-up, quotes; one business moves across) → 04 "תזכורת בטלגרם" (10:00,
what to say) → 05 "כל בוקר מחדש" (daily scan) → payoff wide shot → end card.

Three hook options the owner is choosing between:
- A. Question: "מאיפה יגיעו הלקוחות של החודש הבא?" over an empty board, answered at the end.
- B. Anticipation: no text for 2 s; a dark city map, a scan line sweeps, pins light up one by one; at ~3 s "9 נמצאו · 4 חדשים".
- C. Pain: a messy list of businesses full of question marks, "למי כבר התקשרתי? מתי לחזור?" (mirrors the sister film's missed calls).

## What to send back
1. Which hook you'd choose (A, B, C or a better one of your own) and why, in 3-4 sentences of English.
2. The full script as a table: seconds | scene | on-screen title + sub-line (Hebrew) | voice line (Hebrew).
   Keep every voice line speakable in one breath and true to the facts above.
3. A payoff headline (Hebrew, max 6 words) for the wide shot, in the spirit of "אף שיחה לא נשארת רק מספר.".
4. Two alternative versions of the first voice line.
5. Self-critique in English: the 3 weakest points of your own script and how you'd fix them; any line that risks
   claiming something not in the facts.
