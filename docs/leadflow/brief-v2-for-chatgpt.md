## Facts you may use (nothing else; never invent results, numbers of users or testimonials)

  customer and its project opens ("העבודה מתחילה בדיוק איפה שהמכירה נגמרה").

- **Today ("היום")**: every morning one list of whom to call: overdue call-backs first, then today's, then meetings and
  tasks; a counter of today's calls against your goal.
- **Meeting prep ("הכנה לפגישה")**: before a meeting, the AI reads everything about the lead (details, notes, activity,
  what they said on calls, deals and quotes, their website, what you sell and your prices), asks you a few questions
  about what you want from the meeting, then writes your prep: the questions to ask, the best option (track) for them,
  a plan whose minutes fit the meeting, opening and closing lines, answers to likely objections.
